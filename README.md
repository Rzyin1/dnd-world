## The world

Eryndor is a medieval fantasy setting spanning a mapped area of 1,528 × 794 miles, including surrounding waters. Humans are its only civilized species. Ordinary animals, monsters and mythical creatures exist. Magic is real, and multiple gods form a single shared pantheon.

## Gods and religion

Multiple gods exist within one shared pantheon and can grant divine magic. The number, names and domains of gods, and the religions that worship them, remain undecided. Twelve gods was a suggestion, not canon.
