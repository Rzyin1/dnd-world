01 / Setting

The world

A medieval fantasy world spanning a mapped area of 1,528 × 794 miles, including surrounding waters. Humans are the only civilized species. Ordinary animals, monsters and mythical creatures exist. Magic is real, and multiple gods form one shared pantheon.

04 / Arcane foundations

The four sources of magic

Divine

Granted by gods or divine beings.

Innate

Abilities individuals are born with.

Learned

Acquired through study and training.

Environmental

Drawn from the natural magical energy of the world.

Mechanics, limits and prevalence have not been established.

05 / Divine lore

One shared pantheon

Multiple gods exist and can grant divine magic. Their number, names, domains and associated religions remain undecided. Twelve gods is a proposal, not canon.

06 / In development

Worldbuilding roadmap

  • Verify cultural assignments and settlement geography
  • Define gods and religions
  • Develop magic mechanics and limitations
  • Establish languages, currency, calendar and historical eras
  • Name geographical landmarks
  • Develop royal families, political relationships and kingdom histories
  • Develop monsters and mythical creatures
  • Develop characters and story

Only approved decisions become canon. Generated map defaults and proposed lore remain provisional.
